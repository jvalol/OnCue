OnCue records to ~/Movies/OnCue by default, which is why it carries the Movies read-write entitlement. To see it: open OnCue from the menu bar, choose Record a Test, and a recording appears in that folder within seconds and opens in Finder. The same folder is where Show Recordings goes and where the size limit trims old files.

New in 4.13: Shortcuts actions (Arm OnCue, Disarm OnCue, Is OnCue Armed, Record a Test) and a Focus Filter. The Focus Filter only performs while OnCue is running, so add it to a Focus in System Settings while OnCue is open, turn on its switch, then toggle the Focus.
